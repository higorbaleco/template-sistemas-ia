Pill action button — emerald primary for the single most important action, neutral secondary beside it.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg">Solicitar diagnóstico</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" iconRight={null}>Conhecer soluções</Button>
```

Marketing CTAs pair primary + secondary side by side and keep the trailing arrow on the primary only. In the CRM, use `size="sm"` and `iconRight={null}` for table/row actions.
